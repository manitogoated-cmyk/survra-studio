# Funny case candidates (researched 2026-11-18)

The owner asked for "more entertaining funny stories". These are true, documented, absurd stories where nobody's suffering is the joke.

Every story was checked against **two independent sources** that were fetched and read. Each case is re-checked at authoring, as with the rest of `slate.json`.

The score is out of 100: curiosity, story, payoff and humour, 25 each. They are not in `slate.json` yet.
