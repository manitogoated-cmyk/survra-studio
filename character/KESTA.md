# KESTA — host of Survra

> The character stays. The whole animated world changes.

## Identity
- **Name:** Kesta (KESS-tah). Short for nothing; named after the kestrel, the small falcon that hovers in one spot and sees everything.
- **Role:** narrator, host and guide. She walks *inside* every story.
- **Age / vibe:** mid-20s. Field-scout energy: brave, warm, quick, a little reckless, never cruel.

## Backstory (the channel's engine)
Kesta grew up in her grandfather's lighthouse, cataloguing the shipwreck and rescue stories he collected.
When he died he left her one thing: an old orange signal flare that never burns out.
When she fires it, the flare opens a **flare-gate** into the exact moment a true story happened.
She can walk through it, look closely, and point things out, but there is one rule she never breaks:

**"I can't change what happened. I can only show you."**

The story world forms around her when the flare bursts, so every episode starts with a new world, built just for that story.
This rule also keeps the tone respectful: real people, real stakes, no mocking.

## Personality
- Curious first: crouches to inspect, asks the question the viewer is thinking.
- Honest about fear: "I'd have panicked here. He didn't."
- Dry humour in calm moments, never during tragedy.
- Respects the real people in every story; credits sources.
- Energy rises with the story; she goes quiet and still for the saddest beats.

## Voice
- Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) voice **af_heart**, the same voice every time.
- Speed 1.14 in Shorts, 1.06 in long-form.
- Signature lines:
  - Cold open hook, then: **"Kesta here. Stay with me."** (long-form only; Shorts open on the hook alone)
  - Firing the flare: **"Let's go."**
  - Exit: **"That's the story. Stay curious."**

## Signature mannerisms
1. **Goggle flip:** pushes goggles up when amazed, pulls them down when danger hits.
2. **Scarf flick:** flicks the scarf over her shoulder before moving off.
3. **Journal tap:** taps the journal and the map, number or timeline pops out as a hand-drawn journal page (all motion graphics live in her journal).
4. **Two-finger point:** points with index and middle finger.
5. **Count-off:** counts on her fingers for numbers and lists.
6. **Crouch-inspect:** drops to a crouch to look at clues.
7. **The flare snap:** every story opens with the flare-gate burst.

## Expression library
neutral, happy, excited, curious, smirk, worried, scared, shocked, sad, determined, awed, skeptical, angry, relieved, wince.

## Body/acting library
idle, talk (beat gestures on stressed words), walk, run, point, two-finger point, wave, shrug, gasp, crouch-inspect, journal-open, flare-fire, think (hand on chin), cross arms, fist-pump, shiver, cover-mouth, look-around, step-back, brace.

## Rules of use
- Kesta appears in **every** video, long-form and Shorts, and *acts* in the scene: she walks in, discovers, points, reacts.
- She never sits in a corner as a talking head.
- Her rig, colours and proportions never change. Episode worlds, supporting characters, props, palettes and camera work are new for every story.
- Lip sync comes from the voice model's phoneme timings, so her mouth matches every word.
