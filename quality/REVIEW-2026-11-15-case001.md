# Animation quality review: Case 001, Okene (Short)

Reviewer: Animation Quality Director pass, 2026-11-15. Standard: professional stylized 3D animated entertainment.

## 1. What was inspected
- **Video:** the exact file uploaded as https://youtu.be/oHuAGeKrDQ8 (publish run 43174152100, artifact matches the 07:38 UTC upload). 1080x1920, 30 fps, 49.2 s, H.264 4.0 Mb/s, AAC 48 kHz stereo.
- **Frames:** the whole timeline at 4 frames per second (197 frames on timestamped contact sheets), key frames at full resolution, plus frame-to-frame difference for every one of the 1,476 frames.
- **Audio:** measured, **not listened to**. EBU R128 loudness, loudness range, 0.1 s level windows. Statements about how the voice *sounds* are not made; voice findings are facts about the pipeline.
- **Project files:** `episodes/r001-okene/` (script, storyboard, world3d.py), `engine4/kit3d.py`, `engine3/render3.py`, `engine3/audio3.py`, `engine2/qc.py`, `character/ROOK.md`, `survra-voice-studio/VOICE_IDENTITY.md`.
- **Cross-check:** newest Short, Case 015 Hall (rendered 15 Nov), full contact sheets. Same engine, same core problems; Hall stages Rook inside the scene better.

## 2. Verdict
The ideas are better than the execution. The story beats are good and several visual ideas are strong (depth ruler, rolling cabin, air gauge, TRUE stamp, the diver rocketing up). But the picture is soft and stuttery because it was rendered small and slow. The characters are built from bare primitives and stand in static poses, with faces that cannot show the emotion the narration describes. The sound is a drum loop with no quiet in it. **It looks cheap because of the production system, not because of one bad shot.** Every Short made by this engine had the same ceiling.

## 4. Top five problems
1. **The image is soft and stutters.** Rendered at 432x768 with 8 samples and stretched 2.5x; animation at 15 fps doubled to 30. (Whole video)
2. **Characters don't act.** Poses blend with plain smoothstep between ~10 stock poses; no anticipation, overshoot, breathing or weight. Rook mostly crosses his arms. (4.75 s, 24.25 s, 27.0 s, 32.5 s)
3. **Faces can't show what the story says.** The diver has no mouth and still "screams"; Okene's shock is two white squares. (3.5 s, 16.0 s, 41.75 s)
4. **The editing serves a cut quota, not the story.** QC failed any shot over 2.0 s, so every action is chopped before it lands. The turning point ("Then, a light", 34.25 s) is a tiny dot.
5. **The sound has no dynamics.** A drum loop runs through every beat, tragic ones included; no silence anywhere.

## 6. Strengths to keep
- Recognizable host silhouette (hat, beak, coat) that stays on-model in every shot.
- Story-specific sets for every location; no reused stock backgrounds.
- Diegetic graphics: depth ruler (18.75 s), air gauge (30 s), DAY stamps, case board, the TRUE stamp (46.75 s).
- The rolling cabin (17 s), the blackout eyes (21.75 s), the diver rocketing upward (43 s).
- Captions are synced to the word, with the active word highlighted.
- Every frame was genuinely rendered 3D, not a zoomed still.

## 7. Root causes (production stage, not symptom)
| Symptom | Root cause | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Soft, smeared image | 432x768 render, 8 samples, stretched 2.5x to 1080x1920 | `engine4/kit3d.py` `run()` defaults |
| Stutter on camera moves | 3D rendered at 15 fps (`FPS3 = 15`), each frame shown twice | `engine4/kit3d.py` |
| Blown-out whites | `view_transform = 'Standard'` | `engine4/kit3d.py` `setup()` |
| Plastic, unfinished surfaces | One flat colour per material; sharp primitive edges with nothing to catch light | `kit3d.mat()`, `kit3d.add()` |
| Puppet-like movement | Poses blended with plain smoothstep; no anticipation, overshoot, breathing or weight shift | `kit3d.blend()`, `Rig.apply()` |
| Characters can't emote | Human/diver heads have no mouth or brows the face code can drive; ball hands | `kit3d.human_head`, `diver_head`, `Rig` |
| Rook's personality missing | Signature mannerisms exist only in the bible; the pose library has 10 generic poses | `character/ROOK.md` vs `kit3d.POSES` |
| Chopped editing | QC fails any shot over 2.0 s (mean 1.6 s): a cut quota | `engine2/qc.py` RULES |
| Wall-of-sound mix | Score is a kick/clap/hat loop under every mood; bed ducked only ~11 dB; QC fails any silence over 0.8 s | `engine3/audio3.py`, `engine2/qc.py` |
| Generic voice | Designed Rook voice never approved; pipeline still on a stock TTS voice | `survra-voice-studio` (owner decision pending) |
| Caption collisions/overflow | No safe-width fit, no reserved caption band for inserts | `engine3/render3.py` `captions()`, episode `scenes.py` |
| Same problems every episode | No review of the rendered video against a craft checklist; QC only measures pacing and loudness | process |

## 8. Repair plan (what was changed, branch `quality-upgrade`)
| Problem | Change | Verify by |
|---|---|---|
| Soft/stutter | 60 fps rendered frames, 720x1280 render, adaptive sampling + OIDN (albedo/normal), AgX | Frame-difference profile of the new render; full-res frame comparison |
| Surfaces | Bevel modifier + object-space value/roughness variation on every primitive | Side-by-side stills |
| Acting | `act()` ease with anticipation/overshoot in every `blend()`; breathing and idle sway on all rigs; poses `tap`, `think`, `brace`, `wash`, `scream` | Contact sheet of the q001 render at the listed timecodes |
| Faces | Human mouth on a jaw + brows; diver visor mouth; face code drives them | 3.5 s, 16 s, 41.75 s in q001 |
| Editing | QC shot cap 3.5 s, mean 2.4 s | QC report on q001 |
| Sound | Cinematic bed with section dynamics, deeper ducking, silence allowed up to 1.5 s | Loudness range and quiet-window count on q001 |
| Captions | Fit to 84% width + soft backdrop | 27.75 s and 39.25 s in q001 |
| Render budget | `preview3d.py` for local stills in the owner's Blender; production split into 16 frame jobs | Test-run timings |

Not changed here (need a decision or more work): Rook's voice (owner picks an audition), wave/lightning/water set work, spine v2 for this episode (it is already the rule for new episodes), volumetric light beams.
